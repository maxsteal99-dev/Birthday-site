Our paradise

Phuket, Thailand’s biggest island: warm Andaman sea, limestone islands, temples on the hills and street food on every corner. We are on the west coast in Choeng Thale, a few minutes from Bang Tao beach.

Early November is the end of the rainy season, so expect warm sea (around 29°C), mostly sunny days and the odd tropical shower. The west-coast surf can still be rough, so if the beach shows red flags, our pool is waiting.

Five days in Phuket

Tap a day to open the plan. When we’ve done a day, stamp it in our passport ✈️

Sun 1 NovDay 1

Sawasdee, Phuket 🙏

Land, check in, first dip in our pool, then Phuket’s famous Sunday night market.

Open the plan
  1. ArrivePhuket Airport (HKT) is in the north of the island, roughly half an hour’s drive from the villa.
  2. AfternoonCheck in, unpack, and the first swim in our private pool.
  3. 4 pm – 10 pmSunday Walking Street (“Lard Yai”) on Thalang Road, Phuket Old Town: Sino-Portuguese shophouses, lanterns, live music and street food. It only happens on Sundays, and we land on one! About 40 min drive. Map ↗

Tip: get there before 6 pm, when it gets busiest. Try a roti, satay and fresh coconut ice cream.

Plan B, if we’re too tired: sunset on Bang Tao beach and an easy dinner at Boat Avenue.

Mon 2 NovDay 2

Bang Tao beach day

Spanish latte, sea, sun loungers and a slow sunset on our own beach.

Open the plan
  1. MorningSpanish latte hunt and pastries around Boat Avenue (Little Paris does French pastries). Map ↗
  2. MiddayBang Tao beach: a long stretch of sand about 3 minutes from the villa. Swim only where it’s safe; red flag means pool day.
  3. AfternoonLoungers at Catch Beach Club on Bang Tao (book ahead), or back to the villa pool. Map ↗
  4. Around 6 pmSunset over the Andaman Sea, right from the beach.
  5. EveningDinner at Boat Avenue or Porto de Phuket next door, where the food hall grills the seafood you pick.

Plan B, if it rains: a couple’s Thai massage, then Chillva night market in Phuket Town (open Mon–Sat evenings).

Tue 3 NovDay 3

Phang Nga Bay

A full day among giant limestone islands: sea caves, hidden lagoons and James Bond Island.

Open the plan
  1. EarlyHotel pickup and boat from Ao Po Pier or Royal Phuket Marina. Tours run about 8–10 hours. Check our area (Choeng Thale / Bang Tao) is in the pickup zone when booking.
  2. Hong IslandSea canoe through the caves into the hidden lagoons (canoeing is included on these tours).
  3. James Bond IslandKhao Phing Kan, facing Koh Tapu, the needle-shaped rock from The Man with the Golden Gun.
  4. LunchKoh Panyi, the fishing village built on stilts over the water.
  5. EveningBack at the villa: pool, takeaway and an early night (big day tomorrow 🎂).

Why this and not Phi Phi: Phang Nga Bay is sheltered, while early-November seas on the open west side can still be choppy.

Plan B: if the sea is calm, Phi Phi and Maya Bay by speedboat instead (Maya Bay is open October to July). If the boats are cancelled, swap this day with Day 2.

Thu 5 NovDay 5

Big Buddha and goodbye

One last view over the island, one last Spanish latte, then home.

Open the plan
  1. MorningLast swim in our pool, pack, check out.
  2. If time allowsThe Big Buddha on Nakkerd Hill: a 45-metre white marble Buddha with views over Chalong Bay. Entry is free. Cover shoulders and knees (sarongs at the gate). Map ↗
  3. ThenA farewell Spanish latte, and to the airport. Until next time, Phuket.

Tip: Big Buddha is in the south, about an hour from the airport, so only go if the flight is late enough.
